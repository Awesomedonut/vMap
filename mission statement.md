# vMap — Mission Statement

> **Google Maps for fictional worlds.**

## The Idea

Today's fantasy map tools — Inkarnate, Azgaar's Fantasy Map Generator, Dungeon Alchemist, DungeonFog, Dungeon Scrawl, D&D Beyond Maps — are fundamentally 2D drawing tools. However sophisticated the editor, the end product is a static image: a PNG of a world.

vMap is different. It's a platform where fictional worlds become **navigable like real places**. Instead of drawing a map, you build a world that people can explore the way they explore the real one in Google Maps.

What you create on vMap is a **world model**: a virtual representation of a fictional world, held as real, queryable structure — places, distances, routes, terrain — rather than pixels in an image. A drawing shows you a world; a world model lets you ask it questions and move through it.

## What Makes It Different

- **Navigable, not static.** Zoom from a world overview down to regions, cities, and streets, with the map reacting and changing at every level — responsive, not a fixed image.
- **Measurable.** Calculate real distances and travel times between locations in the world's own units. How long is Winterfell to King's Landing on horseback? The map can tell you.
- **Street view.** Long-term, go beyond the top-down view to street-level exploration of a world.
- **Procedurally generated, then hand-tuned.** Like a Minecraft seed or Azgaar's generator: pick presets, adjust settings, generate a world — then go in and tweak it, or import your own coordinates and maps entirely. Age of Empires vibes, but for worldbuilding.
- **Hosted worlds, Tumblr-style.** Every world lives at its own address — `aeloria.vmap.com`, `westmarch.vmap.com`, `my-dnd-world.vmap.com`. Creators publish a dedicated map site; communities explore it.
- **Community + custom.** Shared community worlds anyone can explore, alongside private custom worlds for individual creators.
- **Your world is yours.** Full ownership of everything you make, with one-click export of the entire world model — the map, the data, the seed. vMap hosts your world; it never owns it.

## Who It's For

**Creators** who need their fictional worlds to be spatially consistent:

- Film and TV directors scoping out a setting
- Video game designers
- Novelists and other writers
- D&D game masters and worldbuilders

**Fans** who want to go deeper into universes they love:

- Lord of the Rings and Game of Thrones communities
- YouTubers and content creators answering questions like "how far is it really from Winterfell to King's Landing?"

## The Pitch

vMap is a platform where fictional worlds become navigable like real places. Creators generate or upload a world, customize it, and publish it as a dedicated map site — and communities explore it just like they would the real world in Google Maps. It's not just a picture of a world; it's a **world model** you can move through.
